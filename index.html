How to Use DropHub

  1. Sign in — use the Google button, or type any username and password and click Connect.
  2. Go to Storage Manager — click it in the menu, or use the button on the Home page.
  3. Add files — drag and drop files onto the box, or click the box to browse your device.
  4. Review your queue — check the file list and total size; remove a file with ✕, or click Clear All to start over.
  5. Upload — click Upload and watch the progress bar. You'll get a confirmation once it's done.
  6. Customize — come back here anytime to switch Light/Dark mode or change the accent color.

Reset
